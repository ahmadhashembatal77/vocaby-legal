Vocaby Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 16, 2026

Vocaby is a language-learning application. This Privacy Policy explains how the current public release handles information.

1. Current release mode

The current public release of Vocaby is intentionally configured as a local-only release. Account sign-in, subscriptions, billing, the online store, remote Daily Words, remote Stories content, cloud translation, server text-to-speech, cloud backup, cloud restore, and other server-backed features are disabled in this release.

2. Information stored on your device

Vocaby stores learning data locally on your device so the app can provide its core functionality. Depending on how you use the app, this may include:

  • Words and translations you add.
  • Sentences and translations you add.
  • Synonyms, examples, explanations, collections, quiz history, learning progress, streaks, goals, and app preferences.
  • Study-language selections and interface preferences.
  • Reminder settings.

This information is stored in the app's local storage/database. In the current local-only release, Vocaby does not upload this learning data to Vocaby servers.

3. Accounts and personal identifiers

The current local-only release does not require an online Vocaby account and does not transmit account credentials to Vocaby servers. Account-related code may remain included in the application for future releases, but those capabilities are disabled in the current public release.

4. Notifications and reminders

If you enable study reminders, Vocaby may request permission to send local notifications. Reminder scheduling and reminder preferences are handled on your device. Notifications are used only for the reminder feature you choose to enable.

5. Analytics, advertising, and tracking

Vocaby does not use advertising tracking in the current release. The iOS privacy manifest declares that the app does not track users.

Vocaby does not sell personal information.

6. Internet access

The application package may include Internet capability for features planned for future releases. In the current local-only release, Vocaby's server-backed product capabilities are disabled by release configuration. Some operating-system or third-party platform components may still perform their own network activity according to their own platform behavior and policies.

7. Data sharing

In the current local-only release, Vocaby does not send your locally created learning library to Vocaby servers and does not share that library with advertisers or data brokers.

If you explicitly use an operating-system sharing or export feature, the selected content is handled by the destination or application you choose. Their privacy practices are outside Vocaby's control.

8. Data retention and deletion

Your local learning data remains on your device until you delete it through the app, clear the app's data where supported, or uninstall the application, subject to operating-system behavior.

Because online accounts are disabled in the current public release, there is no active server account associated with this release that must be deleted remotely.

9. Children's privacy

Vocaby is a general language-learning application and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children through online account services in this local-only release. Parents or guardians should supervise use where required by local law.

10. Security

Vocaby uses platform-provided storage and application sandboxing to help protect local information. No software or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

11. Future features

Future versions may enable accounts, synchronization, cloud backup, remote content, translation, subscriptions, or other online functionality. Before those features are made available publicly, this Privacy Policy will be updated to accurately describe the data collected, the purposes for collection, service providers, retention, deletion, and user choices.

12. Changes to this policy

Vocaby may update this Privacy Policy when the application or its data practices change. The effective date at the top of the policy identifies the latest revision.

13. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the developer support contact published with Vocaby's App Store or Google Play listing.
